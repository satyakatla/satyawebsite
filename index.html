👤 About Me & Background

Satyanarayana, who goes by the moniker Satya, was born on May 4, 1992 in Karimnagar district, Telangana, India. He spent most of his early years in Hyderabad, popularly known as the ‘City of Pearls’. After completing his schooling at Neo Royal High School, his intellectual journey took flight at the prestigious Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras, Chennai, where he earned his Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering in 2014. While his high school years were steeped in computer science, his fascination steadily gravitated toward the rich mathematical beauty of wireless communication and statistical signal processing.

Following graduation, Satya worked as a Research Assistant at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore (July 2014 – August 2015), diving deep into advanced wireless transceiver design. He subsequently moved to the United Kingdom to pursue his Ph.D. in Wireless Communications at the University of Southampton, conducted in close research liaison with InterDigital Europe, London (conferred in 2019).

From November 2019 to July 2022, Satya was at InterDigital, where he led multiple fundamental inventions and standardisation contributions hinged on 3GPP Release 18/18+ and IEEE 802.11bf (WLAN Sensing). During this time, he co-authored over 20 peer-reviewed papers and contributed to approximately 20 patent applications focused on 5G New Radio (5G NR).

Between July 2022 and May 2025, Satya served as a Senior Staff Engineer at ALL.SPACE, spearheading channel modelling and real-time digital signal processing (DSP) algorithms implemented in software-defined radios and digital hardware for multi-beam satellite ground terminals operating across Ku and Ka frequency bands.

Currently, Satya is leading the Software-Defined Radio (SDR) design at MDA Space, specifically architecting the Open Radio Unit (O-RU) of the on-board processor for 5G Non-Terrestrial Networks (5G-NTN).

5G-NTN & Satellite Communications

On-board processors, O-RU architectures, non-terrestrial channel propagation modeling, and real-time DSP for multi-orbit Ku/Ka-band satellite terminals.

📡

mmWave & Terahertz (THz) Communications

Ultra-high frequency transceiver algorithms, analog/digital hybrid beamforming architectures, low-resolution ADCs, and spatial multiplexing.

📶

Massive MIMO & Multi-Functional Systems

Precoding, spatial modulation, multi-user detection, and Integrated Sensing and Communications (ISAC / IEEE 802.11bf Wi-Fi sensing).

🧠

Machine Learning for Physical Layer

Deep learning and statistical learning for channel estimation, beam tracking, transceiver optimization, and real-time wireless signal classification.
