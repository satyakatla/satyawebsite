Research

Research Interests

Wireless Communications
OFDM
Spread Spectrum Techniques
Cognitive Radio Networks
Signal Processing

My research lies at the intersection of wireless communications and signal processing. I am particularly fascinated by how modern spectrum-sharing and multi-carrier techniques can address the growing demand for wireless bandwidth while enabling smarter, more adaptive radio systems.

Current Projects

Performance Analysis of OFDM under Multipath Fading Channels Ongoing

Investigating the bit-error-rate (BER) performance of OFDM systems across various fading channel models (Rayleigh, Rician, Nakagami-m) using simulation and analytical techniques. Particular focus on the effect of cyclic prefix length and pilot-based channel estimation.

OFDM  ·  Channel Estimation  ·  Fading Channels

Spectrum Sensing for Cognitive Radio using Spread Spectrum Techniques Ongoing

Exploring energy-detection and matched-filter based spectrum sensing methods in cognitive radio networks. The project studies how spread spectrum waveforms can be leveraged to covertly sense primary user activity in a shared spectrum environment.

Cognitive Radio  ·  Spectrum Sensing  ·  Spread Spectrum

Adaptive Modulation and Coding for Dynamic Spectrum Access Planned

A planned study on adaptive modulation and coding (AMC) schemes that dynamically adjust transmission parameters based on real-time channel quality indicators in dynamic spectrum access (DSA) networks, aiming to maximise spectral efficiency.

AMC  ·  Dynamic Spectrum Access  ·  Cross-layer Design

Research Groups & Affiliation

Department of Electrical Engineering, IIT Madras

I am a senior undergraduate student affiliated with the Department of Electrical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras), Adyar, Chennai — 600036, Tamil Nadu, India. My academic work is conducted under the broader umbrella of the department's Communications & Signal Processing research activities.

Future Plans

After completing my B.Tech, I intend to pursue a master's programme in wireless communications or signal processing, deepening my understanding of the theoretical and applied aspects of modern radio systems.
