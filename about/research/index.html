Research

Research Interests

Wireless Communications OFDM Spread Spectrum Cognitive Radio Signal Processing

My primary research interest lies in wireless communication systems, with a focus on spectral efficiency and adaptive techniques. I am particularly fascinated by the challenges of interference management, dynamic spectrum access, and the design of robust waveforms for next-generation networks.

Current Projects

Cognitive Radio with OFDM Waveforms

Investigating dynamic spectrum access strategies using OFDM-based cognitive radios. The goal is to design a secondary user system that can opportunistically utilize licensed spectrum without causing harmful interference to primary users.

Ongoing OFDM Cognitive Radio Spectrum Sensing

Spread Spectrum Interference Mitigation

Studying CDMA and DSSS techniques for robust communication in frequency-selective fading channels. Exploring adaptive spreading codes and RAKE receiver architectures to improve system performance.

Ongoing CDMA DSSS Fading Channels

Machine Learning for Spectrum Management

Planned investigation into applying reinforcement learning and deep learning techniques for intelligent spectrum management and channel prediction in heterogeneous wireless networks.

Planned Machine Learning Spectrum
